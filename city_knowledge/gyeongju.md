## Transit from Busan to Gyeongju

### KTX (Primary option)
- **Busan Station → Gyeongju Station**: ~37 min. Trains every 30 min–1 hour.
- **Price**: ₩27,000–₩30,000 per person one-way. Reserve in advance for seated tickets. Walk-up OK but may get standing (unreserved).
- **Frequency**: ~15 daily departures. First ~6AM, last ~9PM.

### Taxi from Busan to Gyeongju
- **Distance**: ~80 km
- **Time**: ~1 hour
- **Cost**: ₩70K–90K one-way (not shared). Expensive for a family but doable as a private car experience.

### Bus (KOWAST / Express Bus)
- **Busan Express Bus Terminal → Gyeongju**: ~1h15m–1h30m
- **Price**: ~₩12,000 per person
- **Note**: Slower than KTX with luggage for family of 4. Not recommended if you have big bags.

## What to Know Before You Go (Aug 31 — weekday)

### Key Attractions on the Day Trip Route

#### Bulguksa Temple
- **Hours**: 9AM–6PM (seasonal). Last entry 5PM.
- **Admission**: FREE (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
- **Terrain**: Stone stairs up to main shrine (~130 steps — Pagoda Hill). Not stroller-friendly on upper sections. Lower garden areas are accessible.
- **Time needed**: 1–1.5 hours for main area, 2+ hours if exploring all halls.
- **Kids**: Beautiful stone pagodas, temple grounds. Less crowded than Seoul temples.

#### Seokguram Grotto
- **Hours**: 9AM–6PM (seasonal). Last entry 5PM.
- **Admission**: ₩3K adult. FREE for kids under 7.
- **Location**: ~10 min bus/bus stop from Bulguksa. Hilltop — short bus ride up.
- **What to see**: Buddhist statue carved inside granite cave. Air-conditioned interior (good relief from August heat).
- **Time needed**: 30–45 min. Small but awe-inspiring.
- **Note**: Requires separate ticket. Can combine with Bulguksa on same morning.

#### Gyeongju National Museum
- **Hours**: 9AM–6PM (last entry 5PM). Closed Mondays (but we're going Aug 31 = Tuesday)
- **Admission**: FREE
- **Terrain**: Flat, fully accessible, very stroller-friendly. Large indoor space with AC.
- **Kids**: Saddle bronco statue (iconic), kids' corner with interactive exhibits. Great backup if weather is bad.
- **Time needed**: 1–2 hours.

#### Gyeongju Historic District (Eastern Area — Daereungwon)
- **Tumuli Park (Gulpot)**: Several grass-covered ancient royal tombs. Flat open area, good for kids to run around. FREE.
- **Warblary Hill Walking Trail**: ~1km flat walk through historic sites. Connects multiple attractions.
- **Time needed**: 1–2 hours combined.

#### National Gugak Center (performance venue)
- Check if any traditional music performances are scheduled during your visit. Usually evening shows.

### Family Considerations (Ages 4 & 6)
- **Walking**: Gyeongju is more spread out than Seoul. Plan taxi between sites (~₩5K–10K each ride, shared).
- **Bulguksa stairs**: The main shrine requires climbing ~130 stone steps. For age 4, you'll need to carry or use a front carrier. Age 6 can manage most steps with breaks.
- **Seokturam bus from Bulguksa**: Small local bus (~₩1,500) goes up the hill. Not accessible for strollers.
- **Luggage**: Can store at Gyeongju Station (coin lockers or staffed). Or take bags on KTX (overhead racks).

## Food Tips — Gyeongju

- **Songpyeon (rice cake)**: Traditional Korean rice cakes available at local shops. Kids usually love these.
- **Gimbap**: Rice roll with various fillings. Ask about ingredients to avoid pork/ham.
- **Bibimbap**: Mixed rice bowl — easily customized, no pork needed. Common and safe option.
