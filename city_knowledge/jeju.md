## Attractions — Verified Hours & Pricing

### Aqua Planet Jeju (Sep 2 — date TBD)
- **Hours**: ~9AM–9PM (seasonal). Last entry typically 1 hour before closing.
- **Admission**: Adult ~₩35K–40K, Child (4–12) ~₩28K–32K. Kids under 3 or under certain height free.
- **Note**: One of Asia's largest aquariums. Built into the cliff by the sea. Multiple galleries: shark tunnel, jellyfish, penguin, dolphin shows.
- **Kids**: Excellent for ages 4 & 6. Interactive touch pools, walking through underwater tunnels. Great indoor activity (climate controlled).
- **Reservation**: Can buy same-day but advance booking online has ~10–15% discount.

### Seongsan Ilchulbong (Sunrise Peak) — Sep 2
- **Hours**: Open year-round. Summit closes ~19:00. Last ascent ~18:00.
- **Admission**: ₩2,000 adult. FREE for kids under 7 or under 100cm.
- **Reservation**: REQUIRED — book in advance via official site. Peak season fills up.
- **Climb**: ~18-minute steep stairway to summit (697 steps). Not stroller-friendly at all. Age 6 can do it; age 4 may need carrying on some sections.
- **Timing**: If doing sunrise, arrive by 4–5AM. Otherwise morning ascent is fine. **CRITICAL**: Must be descending before ~17:00 (closes at 19:00, last ascent 18:00). This is a hard deadline for planning.
- **Views**: Crater lake at top, dramatic ocean cliffs. Photos are stunning.

### Manjanggul Cave — Sep 2 or nearby day
- **Hours**: ~9AM–6PM (seasonal). Last entry varies.
- **Admission**: Adult ~₩2K, Child ~₩1K (very inexpensive)
- **Terrain**: Paved walkways through lava tube tunnel. Fully stroller-accessible on main path. Temperature constant at ~17°C (cool — bring light jacket).
- **Time needed**: 40 min–1 hour for main route. Extended routes available.
- **Kids**: Fascinating dark tunnels with lava formations. Flashlights (phone) make it more exciting. Great contrast to outdoor activities.

### Hallim Park — Sep 4
- **Hours**: ~9AM–6PM (seasonal). Shows run at specific times.
- **Admission**: Adult ~₩18K, Child ~₩12K
- **What's included**: Frigate Bird Museum, marine animal shows (dolphin/orca), park grounds by the sea, Jeolmul Park (coastal walking trail).
- **Kids**: Marine animal shows are very popular with young kids. Coastal walk is flat and scenic.

### Jungmun Shopping Complex / Songshim Ridge Walking Path
- **Songshim Ridge**: Paved wooden boardwalk along Jeju's east coast. ~30–40 min round trip. Flat, ocean views. FREE. Stroller-friendly.
- **Jungmun area**: Upside-down house (₩3K), maze garden (₩2K). Touristy but kids enjoy it.

### Hamdeok Beach
- **Hours**: Open 24 hours (beach)
- **Admission**: FREE
- **Terrain**: Wide sandy beach, shallow water — excellent for young kids. Many restaurants and cafes along the shore.
- **Note**: One of Jeju's most beautiful beaches. Good day for relaxing with kids who want to play in sand/water.

### Jeongbang Waterfall (Jungmun area)
- **Hours**: Open 24 hours
- **Admission**: ₩800 adult
- **Unique feature**: Only waterfall in Korea that flows directly into the ocean. Short walk through forest path (~15 min). Stroller-accessible on main path.

### O'Sulloc Tea Museum
- **Hours**: ~9AM–7PM (seasonal)
- **Admission**: Adult ₩5K, Child ₩3K
- **What to see**: Tea fields, museum exhibits on Korean tea culture, tea cafe with ocean views
- **Kids**: Open tea field is pretty but limited engagement for young kids. Coffee/chocolate cafe area more interesting.

### Jeju Love Garden / Teddy Museum
- **Hours**: ~10AM–6PM (seasonal)
- **Admission**: Adult ~₩10K, Child ~₩7K
- **Note**: More of a photo/instagram spot. Teddy bear themes are cute for kids but short (~45 min visit).

### Sanbanggulsa Cave Temple
- **Hours**: Dawn to dusk (free)
- **Admission**: FREE
- **Terrain**: Coastal cave temple with cliff-side ocean views. Some stairs involved. Not stroller-friendly on upper levels.
- **Note**: Often included in bus tour stops. Can combine with nearby Hamdeok Beach.

## Family Tips — Jeju

### Transportation Options for Families

#### Option A: Rental Car (Recommended for families)
- **Cost**: ~₩50K–80K per day + gas (~₩15K). With family of 4 + luggage, this is the most practical option.
- **Requirements**: International Driving Permit (IDP) required — family HAS a valid IDP available.
- **Parking**: Most attractions have free parking.

#### Option B: Tour Bus / Chartered Car
- **Day tour from Seogwipo**: ₩100K–150K for the whole family per day. Includes driver-guide.
- **Pros**: No driving stress, guide handles logistics. **Cons**: Less flexible with kids' schedule.

#### Option C: Public Bus (Budget option)
- **Jeju City Terminal → Seogwipo**: ~1h, ₩5K–8K
- **Local buses to attractions**: Limited frequency. Some tourist spots have seasonal shuttle buses in summer.
- **Not practical for young kids** — long waits, transfers, no AC sometimes.

### Kids-Specific Jeju Tips

- **Pork is EVERYWHERE on Jeju**: Jeju black pork (흑돼지) is the island's signature dish. Even non-pork menus may have pork broth. Be vigilant about "NO PORK" requests in Korean + point at menu items.
- **Abalones (Haemyeong)**: Seafood staple — safe for kids if not allergic to seafood. Usually served grilled or in rice soup.
- **Green tangerines**: Seasonal Jeju specialty. Kids love them. Available at fruit shops everywhere.
- **Cafes with ocean views**: Jeju has some of Korea's most beautiful ocean-view cafes. Many have children's menus and play areas.

### Indoor Alternatives (for rainy days)
- **Aqua Planet**: All-day indoor activity (mentioned above)
- **Jeju Carbonated Mineral Water Museum**: Unusual but kids find it fun
- **Indoor trampoline parks / adventure factories** in Jungmun area
- **Shopping malls in Seogwipo**: Lotte Outlet Jeju, Shinsegae Department Store

## Known Issues & Gotchas

- **Seongsan Ilchulbong has a HARD closing deadline of 19:00** — last ascent at 18:00. Plan accordingly. This is non-negotiable.
- **Reservations required for Seongsan Ilchulbong** — book well in advance. Peak season (late summer) fills up.
- **Rental car**: Family has IDP — rental car is viable (not just tours). Car = most practical option for families with young kids.
- **Island is smaller than it seems** — north-south drive takes ~1.5–2 hours non-stop. Plan attractions by region to minimize backtracking.
- **September weather**: Typhoon season can still affect flights and outdoor plans. Monitor weather forecasts.
