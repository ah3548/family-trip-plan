# Seoul City Knowledge — Family Trip Aug 22–28, 2026

## Family Profile (Trip-Wide Decision Rules)

### Kids
- **"Show me something" preference** — visual/consumption over interactive. Attraction value > playground time. Prioritize shows/exhibits/beaches over hands-on play areas when planning for engagement value.
- **Can push through a bad day** — Option C: keep going. Kids have hidden reserves beyond the usual fatigue wall. Don't plan bail-out scenarios; they'll power through.
- **No midday naps** — days must sustain active energy all day. One main activity sustainable; two only if one is light/flat.
- **Attention span** — can easily sustain 1+ hour on walking tours with engagement. Not the limiting factor.

### Dining & Shopping
- **Market food + quick stops preferred** over sit-down restaurants for most meals. Faster, lets you stay in the action. Managing hot food on the go is acceptable trade-off.
- Sit-down dinners reserved for special nights or bad weather.
- **Attraction value > shopping**. Markets only worth visiting if kids get something to see/experience, not just buy.

### Schedule Style & Logistics
- **No unstructured/free days** — every day planned out, nothing left to chance.
- Price sensitive but willing to pay for efficiency (shorter transit = fewer stress points). Taxis ₩5K–20K shared = fine; ₩30K+ evaluate carefully.
- Double stroller available but prefer keeping at hotel unless kids flag (but they can push through, so stroller is last resort).
- **Committed to assigned hotels** — no switching mid-trip.

## Neighborhoods & Transit

### Gwanggyo New Town Station (SSH Ikseon Peter Cat Hostel)
- **Address**: Jongno-gu area (near Ikseon-dong Hanok Village)
- **Nearest major station**: Hongik University or Hongdae (Line 2)
- **Airport express (AREX)**: Incheon → Seoul Station (~43 min, ₩9,500) / Express train (~16 min, ₩14,800)
- **Hongik University area**: Youth district, lots of restaurants, pedestrian streets. Walking distance to Hongdae station.

### Songdo District (Yongsan-gu)
- **Songdo Cable Car**: ~₩24K round-trip adult. Can walk from Congeum Park Cloud Walk.
- **Songdo Central Park**: Large open park with water features, flat paths, good for kids. Connected to the cable car station.
- **Congeum Park (Gonggeum Multicultural Library area)**: Metal framework structures over a river. Free. Flat and stroller-friendly. ~30 min walk from Songdo cable car base station.
- **Songdo KAI Center**: Convention center, hosts events. Near the cable car.

### Myeongdong (Myeongdong New Stay Inn area — Sep 5–8)
- **Myeongdong**: Shopping street, lots of food stalls. Crowded, especially evenings. Not stroller-friendly in narrow sections.
- **Namsan/Seoul Tower**: ~₩16K/adult observatory. Cable car ₩9.5K each way. Funicular from station ₩1.8K.
- **Namdaemun Market**: Open 7 days/week. Traditional market, lots of food and goods. Some shops close early (around 6PM).

## Attractions — Verified Hours & Pricing

### Lotte World (Aug 25)
- **Hours**: Typically 10AM–5PM (weekdays), 10AM–6PM (weekends). Check date-specific hours before visit.
- **Tickets**: Adult ~₩55K–₩60K, Child (4–12) ~₩45K–₩50K. Kids under 3 free.
- **Note**: Children's Museum separate entry, cheaper (~₩25K child). Grand Park is FREE and adjacent to Lotte World.
- **Reservation**: Can buy same-day, but advance booking online has discounts.

### Gyeongbokgung Palace (Aug 26)
- **Hours**: 9AM–6PM (seasonal variations). Closed Tuesdays.
- **Admission**: ₩3,000 adult. FREE on Korean Cultural Heritage Day (last Fri of month) and for kids under 7.
- **Reservation**: NOT required — walk-in OK.
- **Changing of the Guard ceremony**: Usually 10AM and 2PM at main gate. Kids love this.
- **Nearby Bukchon Hanok Village**: Free, open always. Steep hills — not stroller-friendly on upper streets.

### COEX Starfield Library
- **Hours**: 10AM–8PM (mall hours)
- **Admission**: FREE
- **Note**: Famous photo spot inside COEX Mall. Underground shopping concourse connects to Samseong station (Line 2/3). Good indoor activity if it rains.

### National Museum of Korea
- **Hours**: 9AM–6PM (Tue–Sun), closed Mondays
- **Admission**: FREE
- **Note**: One of Asia's largest museums. Kids' gallery on lower floors. Very stroller-friendly. Can easily spend 3–4 hours.

### Seoul Children's Grand Park / Grand Park
- **Hours**: Grand Park is free outdoor park, open always. Zoo section ~9AM–6PM.
- **Grand Park admission**: FREE (outdoor areas). Zoo ₩1,500/adult.
- **Location**: Dongdaemun-gu. Line 1/4 Wangsimni station.

### Namsan Seoul Tower
- **Hours**: 10AM–11PM (seasonal)
- **Observatory**: ~₩16K adult
- **Cable car**: ₩9,500 each way from Namsan Station
- **Note**: Worth the trip if weather is clear. Can see entire Seoul on clear days. Sunset visits are best for photos.

### Bukchon Hanok Village
- **Hours**: Free, open always (residential area — be respectful)
- **Admission**: FREE
- **Terrain**: Steep hills and narrow alleys on upper streets. Lower paths near Seonggyungwan are flat.
- **Nearby**: Seokbol Museum, Jongmyo Shrine (₩1,000).

### Namdaemun Market
- **Hours**: Open 7 days/week. Most shops 9AM–6PM. Some open later for food.
- **Admission**: FREE
- **Food area**: Main alley has lots of street food — hotteok, tteokbokki, odeng, bindaetteok (mung bean pancake).

### Gwangjang Market
- **Hours**: Open 7 days/week. ~6AM–7PM (food stalls may close earlier)
- **Admission**: FREE
- **Food**: Most famous for Korean street food — MAYAK gimbap (₩4,500), bindaetteok, raw fish market floor.

### Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP)
- **Hours**: ~10AM–11PM (exhibitions may vary)
- **Admission**: FREE (exterior and main areas). Special exhibitions charged separately.
- **Terrain**: Completely flat, futuristic design. Good for kids to explore.

### Seoul Forest
- **Hours**: Open always (outdoor), visitor center ~9AM–6PM
- **Admission**: FREE (outdoor park). Visitor center ₩1,000.
- **Terrain**: Flat paths, river walk, small deer enclosure. Very stroller-friendly.

### National Children's Science Center
- **Hours**: 9:30AM–6:30PM. Closed Mondays/Tuesdays (check)
- **Tickets**: Adult ₩1,500, Child ₩1,000
- **Note**: Hands-on science exhibits. Great for ages 4 & 6 if weather is bad.

### DMZ Tour (date TBD — book by Aug 19)
- **Hours**: Tours typically start ~8:30AM or 9AM, return ~2–3PM
- **Price**: ₩55K/adult (~₩220K for family of 4)
- **Booking**: Must book at least 72 hours (3 days) in advance via tour company
- **Age**: Kids under 8 may find it boring/tedious. Bring tablets/activities.

## Family Tips — Seoul

- **Subway**: Very stroller-friendly at major stations (elevators). Smaller stations may have stairs only. Check with station staff.
- **Taxis**: Blue = standard (~₩3,800 base fare for first 2km). Green = premium. Purple = women-only (female drivers/passengers only).
- **Indoor alternatives**: COEX Mall, Lotte Department Store underground, Shinsegae Dept Store, Namdaemun covered alleys
- **Metro card**: T-Money card at any convenience store (~₩2,500 refundable deposit + load amount). Reload at machines.
- **Kid-friendly restaurants**: Korean BBQ places usually have side dishes (banchan) kids can eat — lettuce wraps without pork, egg rolls, sweet potatoes.

## Known Issues & Gotchas

- **No pork everywhere**: Even seemingly innocent foods may contain pork — kimchi stew often has pork broth, tteokbokki sometimes uses pork powder, kimbap frequently has ham/spam. Always ask "돼지 NOT" (does this have pig?).
- **Gyeongbokgung is closed Tuesdays** — plan other activities that day
- **Namdaemun and Gwangjang markets are open 7 days** — no Monday closures
- **Many temples close early** (usually by 5PM) and some may be closed on specific lunar calendar days
- **August humidity**: 30°C+ with high humidity. Plan indoor breaks between 1PM–3PM.
